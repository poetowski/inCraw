What the statuses mean
  • Open to work: LinkedIn shows the #OpenToWork photo frame or the "Open to work" card to all members.
  • Likely: the person says so in their headline ("looking for new opportunities", "otwarty na oferty"…), but hasn't turned on LinkedIn's own flag publicly.
  • Not detected: nothing public. The person may still be "Open to work" for recruiters only, which only LinkedIn Recruiter seats can see.
  • Ambiguous: several people match a name. Pick the right one to check it.

Checks run one at a time with a random 8–20 s pause and a daily limit, so your account looks like a person browsing. Results less than 7 days old are reused.
